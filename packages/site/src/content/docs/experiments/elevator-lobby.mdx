This is the first piece of the elevator scene: one floor, the old hand-drawn
lobby with its big floor number, a call button, a lift position indicator, and
a cab tucked behind its doors. The doors open and close and the call button
changes from grey to white when pressed so we can check the composition before
adding dogs, floor changes, or lift behaviour. Until then, the indicator always
shows the lift at floor 12, going down.

This preview is cropped to one floor. The scrolling camera and glimpses of
neighbouring floors will come later; this isn't the final camera framing.
