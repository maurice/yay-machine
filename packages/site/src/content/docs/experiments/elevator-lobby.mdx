The lift starts at floor 2. Use **Up one floor** or **Down one floor** to ride
between neighbouring floors. The existing elevator state machine controls the
trip and landing-door cycle while the building scrolls past a centered cab.
Every floor has its own doors; they stay closed while the lift is travelling.

The view shows one full lobby and partial floors above and below. The wall
position indicators are still static art samples showing floor 12, going down;
the status below the scene reports the actual lift state. The call button is
still a visual toggle.
